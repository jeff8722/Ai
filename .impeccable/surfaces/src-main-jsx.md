# VESTIQ fitting app (src/main.jsx)

Scope: the whole in-store app (welcome, consent, camera, catalogue, processing, result, staff catalogue). Mode: Persuade on the welcome screen; the flow screens inherit the world and work as Operate screens (large targets, short words, landscape laptop/tablet first).

Audience and job: a shopper handed a laptop or tablet by staff; one photo, try real stock on, shortlist, show staff the code. Staff manage the catalogue behind a PIN.

Constraints: honest AI-preview labelling; no invented prices, testimonials or store facts; price 0 shows as "ask staff"; footwear cannot be tried on; sessions clear on finish and on idle.

User-pinned references: landonorris.com (UI), ../compassion worship center church (heavy display type, chamfered CTA, letter-swap, magnetic, scroll and text motion), fat or multi-colour type kept professional.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every garment and every look is a swing tag on a thread. Refuses the category default of a white grid of rounded product cards under a model-photo hero.

OWN-WORLD: Optical-white tag card stock hanging on red thread over a cool board-grey ground. Woven-black label bands carry chrome. Tag stocks are colour-coded by department: cobalt men, fuchsia women, sun-yellow kids, kraft footwear. Punched holes with eyelets, perforated tear-off stubs, Code 39 barcodes of real product codes, running-stitch rules. Bricolage Grotesque 800 condensed for display, same family for text.

STORY: The shopper sees their shop's real rail, learns one photo lets them try any tag on, starts, picks one item or a top-and-bottom outfit, sees themselves, compares and keeps looks, and shows the tag's barcode to staff.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Woven-black fascia band on top. A steel rail across the upper third. Three huge colour tags hang from red threads, one word each, TRY / IT / ON., over the left two-thirds; real catalogue garments hang beside them on the same rail. Under the tags at left: a one-line promise and the primary tag-shaped button "Start fitting".

FORM: Hang-Tag (swing tags, woven labels, size tabs). Impeccable's pick, position 1 of my grounded list. Seed key dadb2ddc. Signature interaction: tags sway with scroll velocity and swing when touched; a finished look drops in as a tag on its thread; progress is a running stitch.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
